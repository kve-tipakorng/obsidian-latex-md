Thermal Derating of Toroidal Inductors Under Forced Convection

This note summarises the derating measurements taken on the winding line during the third quarter. The intent is to give the quoting team a defensible current rating for parts that share the same core family but differ in wire gauge and turn count.

Introduction

Inductor ratings quoted to customers have historically been taken from the core vendor's datasheet, which assumes still air at 25 degrees. Parts installed in enclosures with a fan see a markedly different thermal environment, and the datasheet figure is conservative by a wide margin.

Measurement Setup

Each sample was mounted on a standard test board and driven at a fixed ripple current while a thermocouple recorded the surface temperature of the winding.

Airflow calibration

Airflow was set with a hot-wire anemometer placed 20 mm upstream of the part.

Results

The measured rise followed the expected square-law dependence on current. See the list below for the main observations.

  • Forced air roughly halves the temperature rise.
  • Heavier gauge parts benefit less in relative terms.
